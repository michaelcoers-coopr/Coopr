# Tiger playtest — High Cedars, build 2372b4f

Imagined persona. These are not quotes from Tiger Woods. The walk is what a tour-level player would ask a caddie for, played on a phone at 390×844, using this golfer’s own bag. The driver stock on this phone is a total near 213 yards, not a tour 320. That refusal to invent a number is the part worth keeping. The notes below are about speed, trust, and missing explanations. Each one is translated into something a 75-year-old can use and a 15-year-old will still enjoy. COOPR stays a fun casual round, not a tournament desk.

The screen he opened on hole 1, before any yardage, was: map, card line `Par 5 · SI 7 · Card 484`, shot `—`, then a paragraph that no hazard is tagged, then `Carry — · Total —`, then the shot box, then wind, elevation, and temperature, then Fairway / Left / Right / Short / Hazard under all of that, then flight boxes that are blank on purpose, then Score with no putt count.

## Hole by hole

**1. Par 5, 484, SI 7.** Stinger off the tee is a shape, not a second stock. He types a number he intends to hit, not the card 484. At 160 the phone calls 7-iron, total 154, “6 yards short of the shot. Calling the total.” Carry is a dash. The 5-iron in this bag sits nearer that number and is never mentioned. Fairway is a scroll below Head, Tail, Cross, Uphill, Downhill, three inputs, and Read weather. Wind typed here dies when he leaves the hole. *For everyone:* say why the nearer club stayed out, and keep the wind for the round.

**2. Par 3, 195, SI 9.** One shot, then the green. 195 is not within 15 yards of the 7-iron 154 or the scattered 5-iron near 162, so the honest call is No club. He can trust that. What he cannot trust is a plays-like he has to rebuild after checking the bag. The green is unverified, so front, center, and back stay hidden. That is correct. He still wants the putt count when he is on the green, separate from the score. *For everyone:* one weather setting for the day, and putts that do not change the gross.

**3. Par 4, 299, SI 15.** Short hole. Aggressive line is a fairway finder, then a wedge. The phone will not draw a carry line because no hazard is tagged to the hole. Lay-up versus go stays quiet until someone types a carry line. That silence is honest. The box that would take the line sits above the result chips, so the decision and the result are far apart. Inside 40 yards with an empty wedge card the phone says “No wedge carry is stored” and offers no way to the card. *For everyone:* the result sits with the club, and an empty wedge card is one tap away.

**4. Par 4, 399, SI 3.** Two full swings. Tee number, then an approach. After Fairway he still has to scroll back up to type the next shot, and the flight boxes are in the way even when he has nothing to write. The long reason (median, sample, confidence, longest is not the stock) is the only sentence under the club. A kid might love the detail. A 75-year-old needs the club and the gap, with the paragraph one tap away. *For everyone:* short line first, Why behind a chip, flight yards only when he asks.

**5. Par 5, 524, SI 5.** Three-shot hole if he plays the bag he has. Go or lay up needs a carry number he types, because the water is not tagged. Totals must not clear a hazard, and the phone already says so when carry is unknown. Good. He wants that sentence next to the club, not after the weather. *For everyone:* lay-up stays a real carry check, and the inputs for wind sit with the club so the number and the adjustment are one look.

**6. Par 4, 314, SI 11.** Another short four. A stinger or a fairway wood is still just “type the shot.” There is no invented stinger stock, and there should not be. The miss pattern he wants (“you’ve been missing this club left”) does not exist until he tags shots, and even then the phone never says it. *For everyone:* one plain miss line from tags he actually made. No aim offset until a yardage exists, and none is invented.

**7. Par 4, 333, SI 13.** Same shape as 6. The slow part is repeating wind, feet, and temperature that have not changed since the first tee. *For everyone:* those three persist until New round.

**8. Par 3, 170, SI 17.** Mid-iron tee shot. If the call is a total, he needs to hear that carry was not measured, in those words, plus the longest total in the sample is not the number to plan. The screen only showed `Carry — · Total 154` and a gap. *For everyone:* plain carry words when a carry exists, and a clear “not measured” when it does not.

**9. Par 4, 405, SI 1.** Hardest hole on the card. He wants the club, the gap, and Fairway in the first look, then the score pinned, which it already is. The score stepper has no putts, so a two-putt and a three-putt look the same on the card. *For everyone:* putts on the footer and on the card, blanks stay blank, zero means a chip-in.

**10. Par 5, 517, SI 4.** Back-nine opener. Same wind as the front if the day has not changed. Leaving the app and coming back should still show Head 8, 12 feet up, 59 degrees, and whether the club is using the shot or plays-like. On this build those boxes reset. *For everyone:* the round remembers the day.

**11. Par 3, 168, SI 16.** Same family as the 8th. A 160-yard number is the 5-iron test again: the tighter 7-iron can win, and the closer 5-iron should be named as left out because the shots scatter, not silently dropped. *For everyone:* that sentence is the trust.

**12. Par 4, 373, SI 8.** Tee, then a mid-iron. After the tee tag he wants the shot box clear for the approach without the tee number copied into flight carry. The phone already refuses that copy. The flight boxes should not sit in the way of the next number. *For everyone:* flight yards stay optional.

**13. Par 3, 148, SI 18.** Short iron or a full wedge. If carry for that club was measured, he wants short, normal, and long from the real P20, stock, and P80, and a line that the long one is not the plan. If dispersion was not measured, say the normal number only. *For everyone:* no ellipse and no width that the data does not have.

**14. Par 4, 336, SI 12.** Position off the tee. Lay-up logic is fine when he types a carry line. The result of the tee shot should show on the card later as the last tag (fairway, left, right, short, hazard), not as a fairways-hit percentage from one hole. *For everyone:* the card shows the tag. It does not invent a rate.

**15. Par 4, 306, SI 14.** Wedge hole if the drive is home. Empty matrix: “No club” and a sentence. He should be able to open the wedge card, type a half carry, and come back. A stored half that is within 15 yards should keep winning, which it already does. *For everyone:* the button is only when nothing is stored.

**16. Par 4, 405, SI 2.** Another long four. The full reason is still one tap, not the headline. The headline is the club and how far it sits from the shot. *For everyone:* Why is optional.

**17. Par 4, 370, SI 10.** Coming in. Score and previous-hole strokes are already pinned, which is the right speed. Putts belong in that same footer, with the words “Putts are not added to the score.” *For everyone:* the footer is score, putts, previous hole, next hole.

**18. Par 5, 514, SI 6.** He finishes, sees “Round is in. Gross N.” and opens the card. Front nine and back nine stay. He wants putts summed only over holes that have a number (“Putts 12 over 6 holes”), and the last result or a dash. He does not want a strokes-gained page. New round clears scores, putts, wind, and tags, and leaves the wedge carries. *For everyone:* the card is a card.

## What already earned trust

- A shot of 0, a blank, or 600 is not stored as a distance. 600 says no club and mentions the 500-yard plays-like limit.
- Carry and total stay different. A total does not clear a hazard.
- The club uses the typed shot until he switches to plays-like. Switching units does not rescale an unchanged shot.
- Inside 40 yards a full-swing stock is not used. A stored wedge carry is.
- Greens stay unverified until someone verifies them. Untagged hazards do not grow a fake carry line.
- The funny line stays off this screen. The play screen is direct.
- Previous hole, a score edit that clears that hole’s debrief, and “Round is in” on 18 already work.

## Ranked top 10

These are the tour notes turned into round speed for every golfer. They are the list to build. Nothing here is a tournament feature.

1. **Say why a closer club was left out.** On a 160-yard shot the phone calls 7-iron at 154 and never mentions the 5-iron that is only a couple of yards off. The 5-iron’s ten shots scatter, so it loses. The winner stays the 7-iron. The sentence has to say that. A thin club with only a few shots gets named too, and it is still not called.
2. **Keep the day on the round.** Wind, elevation, temperature, and “club uses the shot” versus “club uses plays-like” survive the next hole and an app restart. New round clears them. Old saves that have none of this still open.
3. **Count putts beside the score.** Zero is a chip-in. Minus from zero clears the putt back to unknown. Plus from a blank starts at one. The gross does not move. The card sums only the holes that have a number.
4. **Put the result with the club.** Fairway, Left, Right, Short, and Hazard sit under the club, in the first look. The map stays, a bit shorter, so the number, the club, and the result fit a phone.
5. **Say the real carry in plain words.** When a carry was measured: short, normal, long, and the long one is not the plan. When the ends were not measured: the normal number only. When no carry exists: “Carry was not measured.” The longest total, when it is a real longest and not the stock, is labeled “not the number to plan.”
6. **One tap when the wedge card is empty.** “No wedge carry is stored” grows a button, Enter wedge carries. No partial is invented. A cell within 15 yards still wins on its own.
7. **The card shows putts and the last tag.** No fairways-hit percentage and no greens-in-regulation from a partial round.
8. **Short line first, the paragraph behind Why.** The gap and the closer-club sentence stay on screen. The median, the sample, and the confidence sit behind Why.
9. **Plays-like sits with the club.** Head, Tail, Cross, Uphill, Downhill, and the three inputs move up with the call. The club still uses the raw shot until he switches. The map keeps Shot and Plays like as two numbers.
10. **One miss line from tags he made.** Side only, from real tags of the called club. If a side shows up and no yard offset was stored: “No yard aim yet.” If there are not enough misses: “Not enough misses yet.” No aim number is invented.

## Left alone on purpose

Apple satellite and haptics are not on this computer. Front, center, and back stay hidden until a green is verified. High Cedars hazards are not tagged to holes, so the live carry line stays empty until he types one. There is no separate green-reading map. There is no stinger or partial-swing stock beyond the wedge matrix. Voice and music stay untouched.
